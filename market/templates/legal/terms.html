3. Buying

  • You don't need an account to buy. When you send an order you give your name, phone/WhatsApp number and area so the Seller can contact you.
  • Sending an order is a request to buy. It becomes a confirmed sale only when the Seller confirms it with you.
  • Stock is shown in real time but can change quickly. If a piece sells out before the Seller confirms, they will tell you.
  • If your cart has pieces from several Sellers, each Seller receives a separate order.
  • Please only send orders you intend to complete. Repeated no-shows or fake orders may lead to your number being blocked.

4. Prices, currency and payment

  • Sellers set their prices in US dollars (US$). The Liberian-dollar (L$) amount shown beside each price is an estimate using the exchange rate displayed on the Platform. The amount to pay is the one you agree with the Seller.
  • Payment is made directly to the Seller — usually cash at handoff, or mobile money (such as MTN MoMo or Orange Money) where the Seller asks for it.
  • Some Sellers ask for a deposit before starting custom work. The deposit amount and the number to pay are shown on the Seller's shop. Deposits are paid to the Seller, not to us; refunds of deposits are a matter between you and the Seller.
  • Never send money to a number that isn't shown on the Seller's shop page, and never share your mobile-money PIN with anyone.

5. Custom and made-to-order pieces

Custom requests (your photos, measurements, colours and deadline) and “Made to order” pieces are handmade for you. Sellers may limit how many custom orders they accept each week. A custom order is only accepted once the Seller confirms the price, timeline and any deposit with you. Because custom pieces are made to your specification, they may not be returnable — agree this with the Seller before work starts.

6. Pickup, delivery and meetups

Sellers may list public pickup spots and days, or offer delivery. You choose at checkout and confirm the details with the Seller. Meetups are arranged between Buyer and Seller; please follow our Meetup Safety Guide. We are not present at handoffs and are not responsible for what happens at them.

7. Reviews

Sellers may not offer rewards for reviews or post reviews of their own shop.
