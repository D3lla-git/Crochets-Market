1. In short

The marketplace works with essential storage only. Advertising cookies from Google are used only if you choose “Accept all” in the cookie banner. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

3. Advertising (only with your consent)

If you accept, Google AdSense loads adverts on public pages and may set or read cookies (for example __gads, __gpi, IDE) on your device to show relevant adverts, limit how often you see the same advert, measure performance and prevent fraud. Google is responsible for these cookies; see how Google uses cookies in advertising. You can also manage personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

If you choose “Essential only”, adverts aren't loaded at all.

4. Controlling cookies

  • Use Cookie settings to change your choice.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything stored on your device (your cart and favourites will be emptied).
  • Most browsers can block third-party cookies entirely.
