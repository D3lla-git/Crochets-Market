If you ever feel unsafe, leave and contact the Liberia National Police on 911. Then tell us at {{ shop.email }} so we can act on the account.

Where to meet

  • Choose one of the maker's listed public pickup spots — a busy market entrance, supermarket, mall, campus gate, landmark or delivery hub.
  • Meet in daylight where possible. Avoid isolated places, private homes and moving vehicles.
  • Bring a friend if you can, and tell someone where you're going and when.

Before you go

  • Keep all messages about the order in WhatsApp, and quote your order number (it starts with CM-).
  • Check the maker's badges: Verified phone, completed handoffs and reviews from verified buyers.
  • Confirm the piece, size, colour, price (in US$ or L$) and meetup time in writing.

Paying safely

  • Inspect the piece before you pay.
  • Only pay a deposit when the maker's shop page says one is required, and only to the MTN MoMo or Orange Money number shown there.
  • Never share your mobile-money PIN or one-time codes — no genuine buyer or seller will ask for them.
  • Keep your mobile-money confirmation message.
  • Count cash carefully and keep large amounts out of sight.

For makers

  • List only public pickup spots and days you can really be there.
  • Bring only the pieces for that order.
  • Mark the order Delivered in your dashboard after the handoff, so the buyer can review you and your handoff count grows.

Report a problem

Email {{ shop.email }} with the order number, the shop name and what happened. We review every report and may suspend accounts that put others at risk.
